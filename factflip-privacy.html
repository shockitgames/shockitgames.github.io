FactFlip Privacy Policy

Publisher and contact

FactFlip is published by Shockit Games. For privacy or support questions, contact shockitgames@gmail.com. Policy updated: 9 November 2026.

Local gameplay data

FactFlip stores scores, stars, answer-attempt statistics, saved facts, preferences and unfinished rounds on your device. The game has no account system, advertising, analytics SDK or developer-operated cloud synchronization. It does not automatically send gameplay data to Shockit Games.

Offline play and external links

Cards, explanations, artwork and sounds work offline. Choosing a source, policy or support link opens an external browser. Those websites and your browser process data under their own policies. Source publishers do not endorse FactFlip.

Clipboard and support

Copy controls put only the chosen report, source link or support address on your clipboard. No report is sent automatically. If you email support, Shockit Games receives the information you choose to send and uses it to respond, investigate the issue and maintain related correspondence while needed for that purpose. You can request deletion of support correspondence at shockitgames@gmail.com, subject to applicable retention obligations.

Reset, removal and backup

Reset Saved Progress removes local game and learning progress but keeps settings. Clear app data in Android settings to remove settings and saves, or uninstall the app. The release export disables Android app backup; manufacturer transfers or device behavior may differ. There is no in-game cloud restore.

Audience and future changes

FactFlip is intended for a general audience aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has sent personal information to support, contact us. This policy will be updated if data practices change. Google Play and device services have their own privacy policies.
