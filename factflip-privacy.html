FactFlip Privacy Policy

Publisher and version scope

FactFlip is published by Shockit Games. Contact shockitgames@gmail.com for privacy or support questions. Updated: 10 November 2026. Version 1.0 is an offline build without advertising. Version 1.1 adds Curiosity Coins, cosmetics and Google advertising integration; the development package uses test ad units until publisher configuration and release testing are complete. This policy describes the features available in your installed version.

Local gameplay and customisation data

FactFlip stores scores, level stars, answer-attempt statistics, saved facts, unfinished-round checkpoints, settings, Curiosity Coin balances, owned/equipped cosmetics, reward receipts, ad-frequency counters and an age-range preference on your device. A birthday is not requested. The game has no account system or developer-operated cloud synchronization. Quiz answers, scores and your cosmetic collection are not automatically submitted to Shockit Games.

Google advertising in version 1.1 and later

The advertising-enabled build includes Google Mobile Ads and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP), integrated through the Poing Studios plugin. It supports optional rewarded ads for cosmetic coins and occasional interstitial ads at level breaks. Google advertising services may collect and share IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), ad/device or account-related identifiers, app/ad interactions and diagnostic/performance information for advertising, measurement, analytics and fraud prevention. Data handling depends on SDK configuration, device settings and privacy choices. The app does not include a separate developer analytics service. Test ads do not make the advertising SDK data-free.

Ad privacy and age preferences

Open Settings → Ad privacy and age preference to review or change the saved age range, retry setup, or open Google Privacy Choices where a form is required. The current game does not request or display ads while the age preference is unknown, under 16, or Prefer not to say. Ages 16–17 receive under-age-of-consent treatment; adult setup checks UMP consent information and presents a form where required. The current integration requests non-personalized ads and limits ad content to PG, but non-personalized ads may still process device data. If privacy setup or an ad is unavailable, quiz play remains available. These choices do not delete information Google may already hold.

Coins and offline play

Curiosity Coins are local, non-transferable cosmetic currency with no cash value. Completing a full round of at least five cards earns coins; optional rewarded ads grant the disclosed amount only after the ad reward event. Quiz cards, explanations, artwork, practice and sources are not locked behind coin purchases. Included quiz content and earned cosmetics work offline; downloading advertisements requires internet.

External links and clipboard

Opening a source, privacy or support link launches your browser. External publishers, GitHub Pages, Google Play, your browser and device services have their own privacy practices. Source publishers do not endorse FactFlip. Copy controls put the selected source, report or support address on your clipboard. Fact and diagnostic reports are not sent automatically.

Support correspondence

If you email Shockit Games, we receive the information you choose to send and use it to respond, investigate the issue and maintain related correspondence while needed for that purpose. You may request deletion of support correspondence at shockitgames@gmail.com, subject to applicable retention obligations. Do not send passwords or signing credentials.

Reset, removal and retention

Reset Saved Progress removes local game/learning progress, scores, level stars and saved facts while retaining settings, Curiosity Coins, cosmetics and ad preferences. Use Android Clear app data or uninstall to remove the app’s local settings and saves as well. The prepared export disables Android app backup; manufacturer transfer or device behavior may differ. There is no in-game cloud restore. Local reset does not delete Google advertising-provider data. You can manage/reset the advertising identifier through supported Android settings and consult Google for its retention/deletion options.

Audience and changes

FactFlip is intended for a general audience aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. Contact us if you believe a child has sent personal information to support. We will update this policy if data practices change. Google privacy information is available at https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Google’s SDK data disclosure is at https://developers.google.com/admob/android/next-gen/privacy/play-data-disclosure.
