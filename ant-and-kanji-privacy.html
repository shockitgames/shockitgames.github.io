Privacy Policy

Developer: Shockit Games
Last updated: 22 October 2026
Contact: shockitgames@gmail.com

1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains information handled by Ant & Kanji, its optional services, and support provided by Shockit Games. The game teaches Japanese through the Ant trail, Kinu’s journey, and Hiragana and Katakana activities. This policy applies specifically to Ant & Kanji; other Shockit Games apps have separate policies.

You can play as a guest without creating a Shockit Games account. We do not ask you for a Google password or email address inside the game. Japanese pronunciation playback is not included in this version. Music and game sound effects can be adjusted in Settings.

2. Information handled

InformationPurpose and handling
Progress and preferencesAnt and Kinu checkpoints, food, inventory, learning results, kana practice and notebook cards are saved on your device. Sound, controls, practice preferences and a coarse age range are also stored locally. We do not store an exact birth date. Android automatic app-data backup is disabled in this release configuration.
Optional Play Games profile and saved gameIf you connect through an available Google Play Games option, Google authenticates your existing Play Games profile. The game uses its player identifier to keep cloud operations with that profile. If you explicitly save, Google receives a snapshot of Ant/Kinu progress, food, inventory and kana learning progress. Advertising-service dataWhen advertising services are used, Google may process IP address and approximate location inferred from it, app/ad interactions, diagnostics and device or account-related identifiers for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. Removing advertising-ID permission and requesting non-personalised ads does not mean all SDK data collection stops.
Support correspondenceIf you email us, we receive your email address, message and information you choose to attach, so we can answer your request.

We do not operate a game-progress or account server. We do not add our own gameplay analytics, chat, public profiles, microphone recording, camera capture or precise-location collection. The current game does not offer in-app purchases.

3. Optional Google Play Games and cloud saves

Guest access keeps learning and story activities available offline. Where cloud connection is available, you choose when to connect, read, save, restore or delete a cloud copy through Account & cloud saves. Cloud operations are manual in this version. Google may authenticate its platform automatically in an enabled Android build; switching to guest stops this game’s cloud actions without signing out your Google account.

A restore replaces the complete local progress copy; currencies are not combined. Local recovery copies help recover from interrupted writes. Google may queue an accepted change offline and synchronise later. An operation already accepted by Google may finish after guest selection or a timeout. Confirm a cloud save on another device before removing your local copy. Google controls service-side retention, account eligibility and supervision settings.

4. Age choices and optional advertising

The game asks for an age range: under 13, 13–17, 18 or older, or prefer not to say. All choices keep access to learning. The game’s advertising bridge is disabled for under-18, unknown, unchosen or unreadable age settings. Only a saved adult choice can authorize it.

Eligible adults may optionally watch a rewarded ad at the anthill for five stored food. Ads are not required for learning or story progression. This version does not show banner, interstitial or app-open ads. We request non-personalised ads, use a general-audience ad rating, disable publisher first-party IDs, and remove Android advertising-ID and AdServices permissions. Google’s SDK may still process the service data described above.

For eligible adults, consent and advertising services may contact Google when initialized, even without watching an ad. Where required, Google’s consent form provides privacy choices. Reopen available options through Settings → Privacy policy & data → Advertising choices. Changing the age setting may require an app restart for advertising changes to apply. Parents and guardians can help children use the learning activities and manage local settings.

5. Providers, security and transfers

We use Google’s Mobile Ads, consent and optional Play Games services for the purposes described above, and our email provider to handle support. We do not sell personal information. Service providers may process information in countries outside yours, according to their policies. No mediated advertising networks are enabled in this release.

Google’s advertising SDK documents encryption in transit using TLS. We rely on provider security for their services; local progress remains subject to your device’s security and backups. No storage or transmission method is guaranteed completely secure. Do not send passwords, authentication tokens or signing keys to support.

Provider information: Google Privacy Policy, Google’s use of information from partner apps, and Google Mobile Ads data disclosures.

6. Retention, deletion and your choices

Local progress remains until you delete it, clear app storage or uninstall. Settings → Privacy policy & data → Delete local game progress removes Ant/Kinu/kana progress and related backup, temporary and restore-journal copies after confirmation. It keeps sound/control preferences and the age choice, and switches the game to guest. Android’s Clear storage option also removes preferences.

Local deletion keeps the cloud copy. A later explicit cloud restore may bring it back. To remove a cloud snapshot, connect the relevant profile, read the slot, resolve any conflicting copies, choose Delete cloud save and confirm. Device progress stays. These actions do not delete your Google account or Play Games profile. Google’s account and privacy controls govern provider-side data and retention.

We retain support messages only as reasonably needed to respond, investigate issues and meet applicable legal obligations; we periodically review and remove messages no longer needed. Google independently controls retention of advertising and service data. You may contact us for privacy access, correction or deletion requests and use Google’s privacy controls. We may need limited information to verify and address a request. Rights vary by location.

Detailed instructions: Ant & Kanji data deletion help.
