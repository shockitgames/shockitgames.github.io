1. About this policy

This policy explains how Shockit Games handles information in the current Android version of Bloom & Bow and through support for the game. It also explains how the privacy and support pages on this website are hosted. Other Shockit Games apps may have their own policies.

The current game does not create a Shockit Games account or provide active Google sign-in or cloud synchronization. If these features are introduced, we will update this policy before they are used to handle additional information.

2. Information handled by the game

InformationPurpose and handling
Game progress and preferencesLevels, scores, stars, coins, owned items, challenge records and audio/accessibility settings are stored on your device to save progress and operate the game. The current app does not upload this save to a Shockit Games server.
Advertising-service dataGoogle’s advertising SDK may process an IP address (including approximate location inferred from it), ad/app interactions, diagnostics, and device or account-related identifiers, depending on configuration and privacy choices. These support ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention.
Privacy choicesGoogle’s consent tools process information needed to determine applicable messages and record or apply your advertising privacy choices.
Support messagesIf you email us, we receive your email address and whatever information or attachments you choose to send. We use this to respond and resolve your request.

The current app does not request access to your contacts, microphone, camera, or precise GPS location for gameplay. Local game statistics are not a separate Shockit Games online analytics service; advertising SDK diagnostics and interactions are described above.

3. Advertising and third-party services

Bloom & Bow integrates Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads and Google’s User Messaging Platform for advertising privacy choices. Watching a rewarded ad is optional. You can play without accepting a reward-ad offer. During development, ads may be Google test ads.

Advertising or consent services may contact Google during initialization, privacy checks or ad loading, before you choose to watch a reward ad. Choosing not to watch an ad does not necessarily stop all SDK communications.

Google processes advertising-service information under its own policies and applicable arrangements. We do not control Google’s independent retention of that information. Read Google’s Privacy Policy, how Google uses information from services that use its technology, and Google’s advertising SDK data disclosures.

We use Gmail to receive and respond to support emails. Google may process email information as the email-service provider. We do not operate a marketplace for selling player information. Advertising-related collection and sharing by Google is described in this policy.

4. Your choices

  • Reward ads: you can skip optional ad offers.
  • Advertising privacy: where required, the game requests applicable privacy choices before loading ads. When Google requires a privacy-options entry point, use Settings → Privacy & Safety → Ad privacy choices to review your choices. Available options depend on your region and the services enabled.
  • Android controls: you can review your device’s advertising privacy settings, including available advertising-ID controls.
  • Local saves: use Settings → Privacy & Safety → Delete Local Progress to reset the game’s stored progress on that device. This is separate from advertising-consent choices.
  • Privacy requests: email us to ask about access, correction, deletion or other rights available under applicable law. You may also be entitled to contact your local data-protection authority. We may need enough information to identify the relevant support record and verify a request.

Deleting local game progress does not delete information held independently by Google or emails already sent to support. Requests concerning Google’s own data can also be made through Google’s privacy tools.

5. Retention and deletion

Game progress remains on your device until you reset it or remove the app’s data. Device backup or restore settings may affect whether app data is restored after reinstallation; review those settings if you want to remove backed-up copies.

We retain support correspondence only as needed to handle the request, maintain necessary support records, resolve disputes and meet applicable legal obligations. You can request deletion by emailing us. If some records must be retained for a legitimate reason, we will explain that when responding.

Google’s advertising, consent and email-service records are retained according to Google’s policies and your applicable choices. We do not promise that resetting the app removes those third-party records.

6. Security and international processing

We take reasonable steps to protect information we handle. Your local save is subject to your device’s security protections. Google states that information collected by its Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit. No storage or transmission system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Support and third-party services may process information in countries other than your own. Their handling and safeguards are described in the relevant provider policies linked here.

7. Intended audience

Bloom & Bow is intended for teenagers and adults aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. Local laws may provide additional protections for older minors. A parent or guardian who believes a child has provided information through our support channel can contact us to request review and appropriate deletion.

8. This website

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not add analytics scripts, advertising scripts, account forms or tracking cookies to this website. The hosting provider may process technical request information, such as IP addresses, to deliver and secure the pages. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. Following an external link or sending an email is governed by the relevant service’s policies as well.

9. Changes to this policy

We will update this page if our practices change. The date above identifies the latest revision. Where required, we will provide additional notice or request choices before using information for a new purpose.

10. Contact Shockit Games

For support and privacy questions about Bloom & Bow, email shockitgames@gmail.com. For a privacy request, include “Bloom & Bow privacy” in the subject and explain what you would like us to review.
