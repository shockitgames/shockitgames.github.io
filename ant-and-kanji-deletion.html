Data Deletion Help

Developer: Shockit Games
Last updated: 22 October 2026
Contact: shockitgames@gmail.com

Delete progress on this device

  1. Open Ant & Kanji → Settings → Privacy policy & data.
  2. Choose Delete local game progress, read the confirmation, and confirm only if you want to remove it.

This removes Ant/Kinu checkpoints, food, inventory, kana results and notebook progress, together with their recovery files. Sound/control preferences and the age choice stay. The game switches to guest. To also remove preferences, use Android Settings → Apps → Ant & Kanji → Storage → Clear storage. Menu names vary by device. Local deletion does not remove a cloud copy.

Delete an optional cloud save

  1. Return to the main menu and open Account & cloud saves.
  2. Connect the Google Play Games profile holding the save, then Read cloud save.
  3. If two copies conflict, choose the copy to keep first. Then read the slot again.
  4. Choose Delete cloud save and confirm. Recheck online to confirm propagation.

Your device copy is kept. This removes this game’s saved-game snapshot, not your Google account or Play Games profile. Cloud controls appear only where the service is available. An operation already accepted by Google may still synchronise after a timeout or guest selection.

If you cannot open the app or need privacy help

Email shockitgames@gmail.com with “Ant & Kanji data deletion” in the subject. Tell us whether you mean local progress, a cloud snapshot or support correspondence. We can guide you through the supported steps; we cannot remotely erase device storage or delete a Google account on your behalf. We may ask for limited information needed to handle the request. Do not send passwords, tokens or private keys.

For Google account, Play Games or advertising-service data, use Google’s data and privacy controls. We do not run a separate Shockit Games account service for this game.

What is retained

Local deletion keeps preferences and the separate cloud copy. Cloud deletion keeps the device copy. Support messages may be retained as reasonably needed to address requests or legal obligations; request deletion by email. Google controls independent provider-side retention. Full details: Ant & Kanji Privacy Policy.
