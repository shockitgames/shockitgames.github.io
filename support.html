Managing your local progress

In the game, open Settings → Privacy & Safety → Delete Local Progress to reset the save on that device. Advertising privacy choices are managed separately where available.

Optional Play Games and cloud saves

In supported builds, open Settings → Privacy & Safety → Guest & Cloud Saves. Connect to Play Games, compare the device and cloud copies, then choose which copy to save or restore. Cloud saves are manual in this update. Use the same Play Games account on both devices and allow time for Google to sync. Do not overwrite a newer cloud copy with an older device save. If connection fails, you can continue levels and CPU duels as a guest.

Delete local or cloud data

Local reset and cloud deletion are separate. Follow the Bloom & Bow deletion guide to remove local progress, cloud saves or request deletion of support messages.

Rewarded ads

Rewarded ads are optional and require a connection and an available ad. The coin reward is granted after the ad service reports completion. If a reward is missing, send the app version, device model and approximate time of the issue; never send passwords. Advertising choices are available under Settings → Privacy & Safety → Ad privacy choices when required by Google for your region.

Game modes

The current release supports campaign levels and duels against the CPU. Online matchmaking is not available. Play Games connection is for supported Google game services and cloud saves, and does not add online multiplayer.

How we handle your message

We use your email address and the information you send to respond to your request. Read the Bloom & Bow privacy policy for details about support information and retention.
