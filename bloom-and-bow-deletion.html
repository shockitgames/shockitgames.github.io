Bloom & Bow · Android

Delete your game data

From Shockit Games · Last updated: 15 November 2026

Device progress and Google cloud progress are separate copies. To remove progress everywhere, delete both. Bloom & Bow does not create a separate Shockit Games account, and these steps do not delete your Google Account.

1. Delete local progress

  1. Open Bloom & Bow on the device.
  2. Go to Settings → Privacy & Safety → Delete Local Progress.
  3. Read the warning and confirm DELETE EVERYTHING.

This resets saved levels, scores, coins, owned items, inventories and challenge/achievement records on that device and removes the game’s local recovery files. Repeat on other devices with a local copy. This does not erase Google’s cloud save, advertising records or support emails.

If you cannot open the game, Android’s app settings offer storage/data clearing. Review Android/device backup settings too: backed-up app data may otherwise be restored later. Clearing data or uninstalling does not itself delete a Play Games cloud save.

2. Delete the Play Games cloud save

In a supported build, connect to the correct Play Games account, then open Settings → Privacy & Safety → Guest & Cloud Saves → Manage / delete cloud saves. Confirm the notice and use Google’s save list to delete the Bloom & Bow cloud copy. Selecting an entry does not restore it in this management screen.

You can also delete data for the game from Google Play Games: open its settings, find Delete Play Games account & data, then delete the individual game’s data under its game-data controls. Labels may vary by device and Google version. See Google’s official game-data deletion instructions.

Cloud deletion does not reset the device copy. Reset local progress on every device as well, and do not upload an old copy again if you want to keep it deleted. Google may retain other service records under its own policies; deleting a cloud save is not a promise that every Google-held record is erased immediately.

3. Request deletion of support information

Email shockitgames@gmail.com with the subject Bloom & Bow data deletion. Identify the support conversation or information you want deleted, preferably from the address used to contact us. We may ask for enough information to verify the request. Do not send a password, verification code, identity document or keystore.

We use the request to locate and delete support information we control. We retain information only where needed for a legal obligation, security, dispute resolution or another applicable lawful reason, and explain any relevant limitation when responding. We do not operate a separate studio account database or hold a server copy of your game progress. We cannot access or delete your Google Account on your behalf; use Google’s controls for Google-managed information.

4. Advertising and privacy choices

Local reset and save deletion do not delete independently held advertising-service data. Review Ad privacy choices in the game where available and Android’s advertising-ID/privacy controls. See Google Account privacy controls for information managed by Google.

For more about information, purposes and retention, read the Bloom & Bow privacy policy or contact support.
